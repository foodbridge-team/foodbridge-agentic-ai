How FoodBridge AI Works

From banquet surplus to community shelter — matching, transit and handover verified in 4 steps.

01
🍲

List Fresh Surplus

Donors post warm meals or bakery surplus with temperature-controlled status, allergen declarations, and pickup expiration window.

⏱️ Safe Expiry Window
02
🤖

Instant AI Match

Deterministic rules screen shelter capacity and dietary rules first, then ranking optimizes transit time to prioritize the best recipient.

🎯 Feasibility Checked
03
🔐

Two-Stage OTP

Donor generates a single-use pickup OTP for the courier; receiving NGO generates a separate delivery OTP upon verifying package seals.

🔒 HMAC-SHA256 Protected
04
🛡️

Tamper-Proof Ledger

Mandatory delivery photo proof and confirmed quantity are recorded onto a SHA-256 hash-chained immutable audit log.

📜 100% Audit Verified
