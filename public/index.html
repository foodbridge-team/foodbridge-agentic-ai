How FoodBridge works

01

Verify partners

Admins review donor and shelter applications before they can publish or reserve food.

02

Match safe food

Eligibility checks consider freshness window, storage, quantity, distance, accepted categories, and allergens.

03

Confirm the handover

A delivery partner or NGO self-pickup records pickup and confirms receipt before an item is marked delivered.
